Welcome to my Artist Statement

This is my artist statement. Ronaldo Angel My work is strongly inspired by video games because Ive loved them my whole life. This shows the kind of art I do; I tend to include things that I am really passionate about and try to improve them or create something new out of them. Most of the time, I will reflect on what I know I love, so things such as landscapes, video games, and anime-type themes, which give me inspiration to create these kinds of art. Every piece I make, or for future art pieces, I connect very strongly to them because I am able to express my love for these things and show my appreciation for others who present similar artwork. To mention something that I often do, is that I have a large imagination but it is difficult for me to express it, so I try to use my art as a way to express my imagination and show others what I am thinking. I also like to use colors that I like, such as blues and greens, which are some of my favorite colors. I just have a large passion for video games so I can to expand my creativity into either making a video game or making art of some. It nothing much but I hope you enjoy what I create and hope maybe inspire others to give me more ideas or make some of your own.
